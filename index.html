About

I'm an Engineering Physics student at Tec de Monterrey (Academic Talent Scholarship, GPA 92/100). Most of my work sits where physics meets a real constraint: a furnace cycle that costs production time, a radiator that has to fit behind a driver, a trade that has to pass a risk budget.

My usual loop is to write the model from first principles, calibrate it against measurements, and then check whether it actually holds up. Each project on this page includes that last step, including the places where the original numbers were wrong.
